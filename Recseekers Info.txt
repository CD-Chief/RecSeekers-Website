Info on Rec2Rec Processes

What RecSeekers is

RecSeekers is a specialist recruitment consultancy that recruits recruiters.

We do not recruit for end companies hiring employees. Instead, we help recruitment agencies hire experienced recruitment professionals such as consultants, managers and directors.

This niche is known as Recruitment to Recruitment, commonly shortened to Rec2Rec.

The problem we solve

Recruitment agencies struggle to hire strong recruiters because:

The best recruiters are rarely applying for jobs

High performers move confidentially rather than publicly

Agencies do not have the time or reach to map competitors properly

Traditional hiring methods attract average applicants rather than proven billers

At the same time, recruiters often feel:

Underpaid compared to what they generate in revenue

Limited in progression or ownership opportunities

Stuck in the wrong market, culture, or leadership setup

Unsure what genuinely better opportunities exist

RecSeekers sits between both sides and solves this disconnect.

What we actually do

We build long term relationships with high performing recruiters and understand:

what they bill

how they operate

their motivations

career goals

ideal working environments

We then introduce them confidentially to recruitment agencies where there is a genuine career upgrade.

The model is selective and relationship driven rather than high volume or transactional.

Who our customers are

Client side (who pays us)

Recruitment agencies hiring:

Recruitment consultants

Senior consultants

Principal consultants

Team leaders

Billing managers

Directors and leadership hires

Typically these are agencies investing in growth, expansion, or new markets.

Candidate side (who we represent)

Recruiters with proven performance, often:

£180k to £1m+ annual billers

Specialists across education, life sciences, technology, cyber, or executive search

Mid to senior level professionals

Candidates never pay RecSeekers.

How RecSeekers makes money

RecSeekers operates mainly on a success fee model:

We introduce a recruiter to an agency

The recruiter accepts an offer

The recruiter starts employment

The agency pays a placement fee based on a percentage of salary

No placement means no fee.

For senior or strategic hires, retained search can also be used.

What makes RecSeekers different

RecSeekers is positioned differently from traditional recruitment businesses.

The business is:

Candidate led rather than vacancy led

Relationship driven rather than transactional

Selective rather than volume focused

Confidential and advisory focused

Built around long term career moves rather than quick placements

We operate closer to a career partner and talent advisor than a traditional recruiter.
